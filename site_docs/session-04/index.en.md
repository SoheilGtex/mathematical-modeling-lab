# Session 04 — Machine Allocation, Oil Refining, and Fractional Optimization

This four-page handwritten lecture note develops **three distinct models**: allocation of three products among four machines (pages 1–2), procurement of two kinds of crude oil for refining (page 3), and maximizing a revenue-to-cost ratio for two manufactured products (page 4).

The handwritten note supplies the **data and formulations**. Numerical optima, derivations and proof certificates below are independent study additions; the source scan is not redistributed.
