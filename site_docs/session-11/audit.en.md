**Why the tuple matters.** A triangular fuzzy number \((l,m,u)\) must have \(l\le m\le u\). If an upper endpoint really were 40 in \((50,55,40)\), it would violate the definition. A source-faithful educational text must flag the discrepancy rather than treat it as a valid triangle.

**Choice of ranking.** The trapezoidal ranking rule \(R_1\) is explicitly written in the source; applying it to a triangle requires **an additional convention** (we state the \((l,m,m,u)\) embedding). Ranking a fuzzy inequality is a further choice. Averaging experts without weights also introduces a choice (equal weights).
