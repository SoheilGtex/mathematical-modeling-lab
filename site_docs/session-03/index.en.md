# Session 03 — Numerical Transportation and Production Planning

The four-page handwritten note develops two linear-programming models: a fully numerical transportation instance (pages 1–2) and a five-month production and inventory problem (pages 3–4). The note formulates these problems; the optimal solutions and proofs on this site are independently derived study material.

## Topics

- [Numerical transportation](transportation.md) — three origins, four destinations, the shipping plan and an exact optimality certificate.
- [Production and inventory](production.md) — regular time, overtime, stock balances, a five-month schedule and a proof of minimum cost.
